## Strategic Garage Website Development Built Around Real Workshop Operations



Garage website design and development should reflect how a workshop actually operates.



Independent garages, MOT centres and auto repair shops manage capacity, scheduling, customer communication and service categories every day. A website must support these operational realities.



That means:



- presenting services clearly and logically

- reducing repetitive phone enquiries

- enabling structured booking or enquiry submission

- communicating professionalism and reliability

- aligning with how customers search and decide



Instead of starting from a visual template, I start from service structure. The website architecture mirrors your core revenue streams — MOT testing, servicing, diagnostics, tyres, brakes, suspension, air conditioning, electrical repairs and other specialisations.



This structured approach ensures clarity for both customers and internal operations.
