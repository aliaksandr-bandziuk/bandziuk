## SEO (search engine optimisation) — indicative pricing



SEO is a long-term process, not a one-off service. Below are realistic price ranges for SEO focused on bringing clients, not just rankings.



### SEO for service businesses and small companies



Price:

- from €800 / month



Minimum cooperation period:

- 3–6 months



Best for:



- service-based businesses

- local or niche companies

- websites expected to generate enquiries and leads



Typical scope includes:



- SEO & technical audit

- technical optimisation

- keyword research and optimisation

- site structure and content improvements

- performance analysis and recommendations



### SEO in competitive industries



Price:

- from €1,300 / month



Minimum cooperation period:

- 6 months



Best for:



- highly competitive markets

- businesses seeking stable, long-term results

- projects requiring advanced technical optimisation



Why it costs more:



- stronger competition

- higher technical workload

- longer time to measurable results



### Full project management



Price:

- €3,000 / month



Best for:



- businesses that want one specialist to own the entire growth channel



What’s included:



- full-time dedicated work on one project — SEO strategy and execution, content, technical improvements and code changes, and marketing coordination

- one person covering what usually requires an SEO specialist, a developer, and a marketer



### One-time SEO services



Price:

- from €250 to €700 (one-off)



Examples:



- SEO audit

- visibility analysis

- consultation and action plan



One-time SEO does not replace an ongoing process, but it is often a smart first step before making a larger investment.



### SEO audit and consultation — the first step before investing



Before committing to long-term SEO or rebuilding your website, it’s important to understand what is not working and where the real growth potential lies. An SEO audit helps avoid unnecessary costs and provides a clear, actionable roadmap.



What the SEO audit includes:



- technical website analysis (speed, errors, structure)

- evaluation of Google visibility and keyword potential

- competitor analysis

- identification of growth-blocking issues

- concrete recommendations — what to do, in what order, and why



No generic reports. No “documents for the drawer”.



Pricing:



- SEO audit: from €250 (one-time)

- Online consultation: free (15–30 minutes)



During the consultation:



- we review your situation

- I assess whether SEO makes sense for your case



I suggest realistic scenarios (SEO, website, landing page, etc.)

## Website development — indicative pricing



Website cost depends not only on design, but mainly on purpose, functionality, and build quality. Below are typical price ranges for websites created with SEO and lead generation in mind, not just “being online”.



### Business website / company website



Price:

- from €2,000



Timeline:

- 2–4 weeks



Best for:



- small and medium businesses

- local services

- companies that need a professional online presence



### Sales-focused / lead-generation website



Price:

- from €2,000



Timeline:

- 3–6 weeks



What makes it different:



- conversion-oriented structure

- strategically written content

- clear calls to action

- integrations (forms, analytics, CRM)



### Website redesign and optimisation



Price:

- from €1,000



Best if:



- your website doesn’t generate enquiries

- SEO or performance issues exist

- the site was built years ago



### Landing page — pricing and when it makes sense



There are two levels, depending on how much technical and SEO/AEO/GEO work you need.



### Simple landing page



Price:

- €700–1,400



Timeline:

- 1–3 weeks



Best for:



- service-based offers

- Google Ads / Meta Ads campaigns

- testing a new service or offer



A focused one-page site on a solid technical base: custom layout, responsive, fast, contact form. For small tasks, campaigns, and testing offers.



### Conversion landing with full SEO/AEO/GEO optimization



Price:

- from €2,000



Timeline:

- 2–3 weeks



Everything in the simple landing plus: structured data (Schema.org), semantic content architecture, AI-search readiness (AEO/GEO), Core Web Vitals optimization, conversion-focused structure and copy guidance. This is the product described on the niche landing pages (e.g., for psychologists).
