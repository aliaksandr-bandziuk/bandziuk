FAQ

Web development, SEO and AI search: questions clients ask

Will the website actually bring me leads?NEW

That is what it is built for. Design, code, SEO and GEO are the means; enquiries and clients are the point. Every project gets clear offers, short forms, contact buttons where buyers decide and conversion tracking, so you can see which pages and channels bring leads. No one can promise a number before seeing your market, but you will know from the audit what is realistic.

How much does a website or platform cost?

A business website starts from €2,000 and a landing page costs €700–1,400. A redesign of an existing site starts from €1,000. Larger platforms with custom integrations are quoted after a short call, and every price is fixed in writing before work starts.

Do you design the website too, or do I need a designer?NEW

I design it. Every project gets a custom interface built for your business and buyers. If you already have a design in Figma, I build it pixel-accurately instead.

What are GEO and AEO, and how are they different from SEO?NEW

SEO gets your pages ranked in Google’s results. GEO, generative engine optimisation, gets your business named and cited when an assistant such as ChatGPT or Perplexity writes an answer. AEO, answer engine optimisation, structures your content so assistants, Google AI Overviews and AI agents can take the exact answer from your page. They overlap, and I build all three into the same site.

Can you get my business recommended by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini or Claude?NEW

No one can guarantee a recommendation. What I can do is make your business easy to identify and verify: consistent entity data, structured markup, directory profiles and pages that answer the questions buyers actually ask. My research on 141 AI answers shows which of those signals each assistant relies on.

Can you redesign my website without losing rankings?NEW

Yes. Before anything changes I map every URL, redirect and piece of structured data, then carry them into the new design. Most traffic lost in a redesign is lost through URLs and markup, not through the design itself.

How much does SEO cost?

An SEO audit starts from €250. Ongoing SEO for a service business starts from €800 a month, and from €1,300 in competitive industries. All prices are on the pricing page.

Should I hire a freelancer or an agency?NEW

An agency makes sense when you need a large team across many channels at once. A freelancer makes sense when you want the person you talk to be the one doing the work. With me, that person designs, builds and optimises, so nothing is lost between departments.

Do you work with clients in the UK, the US and elsewhere?NEW

Yes. I work remotely with clients in 12 countries, including the UK, the US, Canada and Australia. Calls fit European and North American business hours, and contracts and invoices come from my registered EU business.

How long does it take to build a website?

A landing page takes one to three weeks, a business website two to four. Platforms with a CMS, several languages and integrations take longer, and you get a realistic date before work starts.

How long does SEO take to show results?NEW

Technical fixes on an existing site often show in Search Console within weeks. Rankings for competitive terms usually take three to six months, and longer for a new domain. I will tell you at the audit stage which goals are realistic.

What technologies do you use?

Next.js, React, TypeScript, Sanity and WordPress for design and development; Google Search Console, Screaming Frog, Ahrefs and schema.org for SEO; GA4 and Google Tag Manager for tracking; my own AI visibility checks for GEO.

Do you provide contracts, invoices and support after launch?

Yes. I work as a registered sole proprietor in the EU, with contracts and VAT invoices, and offer ongoing development, SEO and AI visibility work after launch. Everything is documented, so you can also take the site over yourself.
