FAQ

SEO consulting and web development: questions clients ask

How much does an SEO consultant cost?NEW

An SEO audit starts from €250. Ongoing SEO for a service business starts from €800 a month, and from €1,300 in competitive industries. A business website starts from €2,000. Every figure is on the pricing page, and your final price is fixed in writing before work starts.

Should I hire a freelance SEO consultant or an agency?NEW

An agency makes sense when you need a large team across many channels at once. A freelance consultant makes sense when you want the person you talk to be the one doing the work, with no margin for layers in between. With me, that person also builds the website, so technical SEO problems are fixed in the code rather than passed between teams.

Do you work with clients in the UK, the US and elsewhere?NEW

Yes. I work remotely with clients in 12 countries, including the UK, the US, Canada and Australia. Calls fit European and North American business hours, and contracts and invoices come from my registered EU business.

How long does SEO take to show results?NEW

Technical fixes on an existing site often show in Search Console within weeks. Rankings for competitive terms usually take three to six months, and longer for a new domain. I will tell you at the audit stage which of your goals are realistic and which are not.

Can you get my business recommended by ChatGPT and other AI assistants?NEW

No one can guarantee a recommendation. What I can do is make your business easy to identify and verify: consistent entity data, structured markup, directory profiles and pages that answer the questions buyers actually ask. My research on 141 AI answers shows which of those signals matter for each assistant.

What is international SEO, and does my multilingual site need it?NEW

International SEO makes each language or country version of your site rank in its own market: a separate URL per language, correct hreflang, localised titles and content, and no duplicate pages competing with each other. If you sell in more than one language, you need it, and it is far cheaper to build in than to fix later.

Why have one person build the website and do the SEO?NEW

Because most SEO problems are created during the build: page structure, URLs, language versions, speed and markup. When the developer and the SEO consultant are the same person, those decisions are made once and made with rankings in mind.

How long does it take to build a website?

A landing page takes one to three weeks, a business website two to four. Larger projects with a CMS, several languages and filtering take longer, and you get a realistic date before work starts.

Can I start with an SEO audit or a consultation only?

Yes. Many clients start with an audit or a one-off consultation and decide on further work afterwards.

Do you provide contracts and invoices?

Yes. I work as a registered sole proprietor in the EU, with contracts and VAT invoices.

What technologies do you use?

Next.js, React, Sanity and WordPress for development; Google Search Console, Screaming Frog, Ahrefs and schema.org for SEO; GA4 and Google Tag Manager for tracking.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes: ongoing SEO, analytics and improvements. Everything is documented, so you can also take the site over yourself.
