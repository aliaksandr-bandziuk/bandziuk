## Post

38 of 40 searches in one niche now end with an AI answer at the top.

I found that out before designing a website for a retail analytics consultant. It changed how the whole site was built.

Three more things the research showed:
→ Demand for the consultant's actual service: single and double figures a month.
→ Far more people search for how to calculate margin or sell-through.
→ The top results belong to agencies and software vendors, not practitioners.

So the site became a tool rather than a brochure:
• 153 pages in English, Polish and Russian, each language written for its own searches
• 5 retail calculators, each a full page with formulas and a worked example
• Excel templates with every formula checked by a script
• 572 questions answered in plain text, because the questions people type are the ones AI assistants get asked
• A design that speaks Excel and Power BI: the contact form is a spreadsheet, tables show a sum when you select numbers

And the part nobody sees: answers in the first sentence, formulas as text rather than images, everything important readable without JavaScript. PageSpeed: 100 on mobile.

On launch day I asked ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google 74 questions from this field. The new site was cited zero times, as expected. I'll run the same questions in October and share the before and after.
