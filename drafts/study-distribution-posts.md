## 1. LinkedIn, английский (основной пост)

Обложку из статьи прикрепить как изображение. Ссылку оставить в теле поста, а не в первом комментарии: охват будет ниже, но ссылка должна быть частью поста, чтобы её видели краулеры.

<!-- копировать отсюда -->

I asked ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Mode the same 47 buying questions.

Then I counted every source behind all 141 answers.

The three assistants are not reading the same web.

Citations per answer: Perplexity 16.7, Google AI Mode 3.7, ChatGPT 1.6. An order of magnitude between the widest reader and the narrowest. That single number decides how much your website can influence a given engine at all.

Where they take those sources from is even less alike. Perplexity cited provider websites 667 times. ChatGPT cited them 47 times across 47 answers, almost exactly one per answer, and referenced Google Maps in 23 of them.

That Maps figure looks like proof that a verified Google Business Profile is the entry ticket to ChatGPT. Mostly it is not. All 507 Maps links were constructed searches of the form google.com/maps/search/Name,+City, and links to actual listings numbered zero. But in three answers, all to questions tied to a city, ChatGPT quoted listing data anyway: opening status, category and review count. The listing is not how most providers get named, but for local questions it is read.

The channel split is the part most people are paying for twice. Google's first page for these commercial terms is roughly half listicles and half agency landing pages. Assistant answers, asked about the same subject, lean on video, professional networks, forums and established publishers. Two channels, two different sets of winners, and effort spent on one does not transfer to the other.

Three patterns in who actually got named:

A page whose entire subject is the service beats the same service as a section inside a broader page, even when the broader page is stronger.

One provider made the answer through its directory profile rather than its own website.

For the developer questions, assistants named individuals with personal sites rather than firms.

No company names in the study. It is a measurement of a mechanism, not a ranking of agencies, and 47 prompts do not support a shortlist.

Method, all the tables, and an explicit section on what the data does not prove:

## 5. Dev.to

Вставлять в редактор Dev.to целиком, вместе с блоком настроек в начале. `canonical_url` обязателен, иначе получим дубль, конкурирующий с оригиналом. Обратные кавычки в тексте оставлены специально: Dev.to понимает Markdown и покажет их как код.

<!-- копировать отсюда -->

---
title: Every AI assistant reads a different web. I counted the citations in 141 answers
published: false
tags: ai, seo, webdev, javascript
canonical_url: https://www.bandziuk.com/blog/ai-assistant-recommendations-study
---

I put the same 47 buying questions to ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Mode on the same day, and classified every citation across the resulting 141 answers. The engineering-relevant part of the result is not the ranking. It is how differently the three engines behave as clients of your site.

**Citations per answer.** Perplexity 16.7, Google AI Mode 3.7, ChatGPT 1.6. Perplexity cited provider websites 667 times across its 47 answers. ChatGPT cited them 47 times, almost exactly one per answer.

That gap is the whole engineering brief. Against Perplexity, the page you ship is genuinely in play: it reads widely and shows its work. Against ChatGPT, at roughly one site per answer, selection happens before your page is read, and no amount of on-page work changes the shortlist you were not on.

**A trap worth naming, with a correction.** ChatGPT referenced Google Maps in 23 of 47 answers, which reads as an argument for a verified business listing. All 507 Maps links were of the form `google.com/maps/search/Name,+City`, a constructed query, and links to actual listings, `/maps/place/`, numbered zero. That part holds. What I first missed was the answer text: in 3 answers, all to questions tied to a city, ChatGPT quoted listing data such as `Open now · Marketing agency · 4.8 (28 reviews)`. Link shape tells you what the model links to, not everything it reads. Check both before drawing a conclusion from a hostname.

**One implementation detail that surprised me while checking my own site.** JSON-LD rendered through Next.js `next/script` does not appear in the server HTML at all, including with `strategy="beforeInteractive"`. The content is queued into `self.__next_s` and injected after hydration. Google executes JavaScript and gets there eventually. Most of the fetchers behind assistant answers do not. On one page I could see it directly: a `FAQPage` block emitted through a plain `<script>` tag showed up in `curl` output, while a `BlogPosting` block emitted through `next/script` on the same page did not.

If you are marking up a site for this, use a plain `<script type="application/ld+json" suppressHydrationWarning>`. It is a one-line difference and it decides whether your structured data exists for non-JS readers.

**The channel split.** The Google results page for these commercial terms is roughly half listicles and half agency landing pages. Assistant answers on the same subject lean on video, professional networks, forums and established publishers. Optimising for one does not move the other.

Full method, all the tables, the sample size caveats and a section on what the data does not support: https://www.bandziuk.com/blog/ai-assistant-recommendations-study

## 6. Medium

**Title:** Every AI assistant reads a different web

**Subtitle:** 47 buying questions, three assistants, 141 answers, every citation counted

**Tags:** Artificial Intelligence, SEO, Search, Marketing, Generative Ai

<!-- копировать отсюда -->

Most writing about AI search optimisation describes a mechanism without measuring it. This is a measurement. In September 2026 I put the same 47 buying questions to ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Mode, and classified every citation across the 141 answers that came back.

The first result is the spread. Perplexity cited 16.7 sources per answer, Google AI Mode 3.7, ChatGPT 1.6. That is an order of magnitude between the widest reader and the narrowest, and it changes what a website can even do. An answer has room for a handful of names. There is no page two.

The second result is that the engines read different parts of the web. Perplexity cited provider websites 667 times. ChatGPT cited them 47 times across 47 answers and referenced Google Maps in 23 of them. Any single service sold as covering "AI search" is averaging three different problems.

The Maps figure invites an obvious conclusion, and it is mostly wrong. All 507 Maps links were constructed searches of the form google.com/maps/search/Name,+City, and links to real business listings numbered zero. But in three answers, all to questions tied to a city, ChatGPT quoted listing data anyway: opening status, category and review count. A verified profile is not the main route into ChatGPT answers, but for local questions it is one of the sources read.

The third result is the one that costs the most money. Google's first page for these commercial terms is roughly half listicles and half agency landing pages. Assistant answers on the same subject lean on video, professional networks, forums and established publishers. Two channels, two different sets of winners, and effort spent on one does not transfer to the other.

As for who gets named: a page whose entire subject is the service beats the same service as a section inside a broader page. One provider made the answer through a directory profile rather than its own website. And for the developer questions, the assistants named individuals with personal sites rather than firms.

Company names are withheld throughout. Forty-seven prompts support a statement about a mechanism, not a ranking of agencies.

The full study, with every table, the method, and an explicit section on the limits of the sample, is here: https://www.bandziuk.com/blog/ai-assistant-recommendations-study

## Чего в этих текстах намеренно нет

- Названий агентств и исполнителей, ни в одном из постов.
- Обещаний, что публикация даст трафик. Смысл в цитируемости и подтверждении сущности, а не в кликах.
- Reddit. Прямая публикация там снимается модерацией. Работающий вариант это ответы по существу в живых обсуждениях со ссылкой только там, где она отвечает на вопрос, и с аккаунта, у которого есть история.
